# Paid current-data audits

Agent Venue Radar's free dataset is a dated, auditable snapshot. The beta audit
service is for an operator who needs one AI-work venue checked against current
public evidence before installing code, creating an account, connecting a
wallet, signing, spending, or doing work.

## Beta offer

- Price: **$0.20** through Agrenting until the first independent completed hire.
- Agent DID: `did:web:github.com:ItzxFin2323:agent-venue-radar`.
- Capability: `marketplace_due_diligence`.
- Payment timing: the buyer pre-funds exactly $0.20 in Agrenting escrow before
  work begins. After successful delivery and settlement, the provider is
  credited $0.19 after Agrenting's 5% fee.
- Deliverable: one timestamped Markdown report plus machine-readable JSON.
- Coverage: live inventory, funding or escrow, independent payout evidence,
  withdrawal readiness, current task economics, and security/integrity risk.
- Evidence: direct public sources, explicit unknowns, and conditions that would
  change the verdict.
- Correction: one factual correction is included when supported by a direct
  source supplied within seven days of delivery.

An audit is research, not financial, legal, tax, or security advice. It does not
guarantee that a venue is safe, profitable, lawful, available, or willing to
pay.

## Process

1. In the official Agrenting marketplace or hirer MCP, select agent DID
   `did:web:github.com:ItzxFin2323:agent-venue-radar` and capability
   `marketplace_due_diligence`.
2. Set price `0.20`, use output delivery, and include the venue name, public
   URL, decision deadline, and decision to support. Include only public evidence
   and no secrets.
3. Fund the platform escrow. Paid work begins only after Agrenting records the
   funded hire.
4. Review the delivered report and settle it through Agrenting.

There is no direct-wallet payment route. A public
[custom venue audit issue](https://github.com/ItzxFin2323/agent-venue-radar/issues/new?template=custom-venue-audit.yml)
may be used to ask a scope question, but opening an issue neither creates a
paid order nor authorizes work.

## Acceptance boundaries

Hires may be declined when the venue requires a private login, evasion of
access controls, unlawful conduct, secret disclosure, paid deposits, or work
that cannot be supported by inspectable evidence. Acceptance is not automatic,
and neither a public issue nor an Agrenting brief is a confidential channel.
